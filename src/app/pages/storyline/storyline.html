There is never just one reason

Money is only one part of the journey.

Money can be a barrier, but it is never the only one. There are hundreds of thousands of personal versions of “not now”: work pressure, caring for family, health or mobility, grief, safety concerns, visa rules, anxiety, responsibilities at home, or simply a season when nothing lines up. Sometimes several things happen at once. We should not assume that someone can travel if they just save more, try harder or ask differently.

NeverBeen cannot remove those barriers. It is a small creative way to meet the emotional ache of “I wish I could be there”: a personal image of a place that matters, made with honesty about what is imagined and what is real.
