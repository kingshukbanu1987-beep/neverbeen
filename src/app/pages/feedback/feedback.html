Feedback & suggestions

Tell us how NeverBeen can be better

NeverBeen is a small studio, and every message lands with the people who build and run it — not in a ticket queue. If something delighted you, say so. If something fell short, say that too. Both are how the service improves.

✍️

What you can share

Appreciation, testimonials, improvement ideas, general or urgent queries, grievances and reviews — about a request, a photograph, pricing, delivery times or anything you think we should do differently.

📲

Where it goes

Straight to our studio WhatsApp, {{ whatsappDisplay }}, as a message written in your own words. Nothing is stored on this site and no account is needed.

💬

What happens next

Urgent queries and grievances are answered first, usually within a working day. Improvement ideas are logged and revisited as the studio grows, and you will always get a reply on the same channel.

Handy to include: your request reference or order date, the destination or package, and a screenshot if something looks wrong — it helps us help you faster.
