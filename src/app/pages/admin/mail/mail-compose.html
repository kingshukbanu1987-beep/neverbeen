How to add admins

  1. Type a name, email, team or role in To or Cc and pick from the suggestions (↑ ↓ Enter).
  2. Paste several addresses separated by commas.
  3. Click To / Cc next to an admin below, or add a whole team.
  4. Drag a chip between To, Cc and Bcc. Backspace removes the last one.
