How this page is fed

Everything here is fetched live by your browser when you open or search this page. Nothing is stored by NeverBeen.

Live today, no key needed

Wikimedia Pageviews for readership, Wikipedia for summaries and related articles, Wikimedia Commons for freely licensed photography, Open-Meteo for current conditions and OpenStreetMap Nominatim to place what you type.

Key-based feeds

Google Places, Instagram and Facebook each require an API key or access token, which a public web page cannot hold anonymously. They are wired in and waiting: add credentials in src/app/models/travel-feeds.ts and their results appear in the feed with a live badge.

Attribution

Photographs are credited to their author and licence with a link to the source file, as the licence requires. Text summaries link back to the article they came from.
