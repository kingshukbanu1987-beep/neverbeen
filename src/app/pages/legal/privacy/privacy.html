1. Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, Facebook or Microsoft we receive only the basics needed to open your account: your display name, avatar and the email address your provider already approved. NeverBeen never sees or stores your identity-provider password.

2. How we use it

Everything we collect has one purpose: making NeverBeen Social Network feel alive and safe.

  • To create your member profile and keep you signed in across sessions.
  • To publish what you choose to share — travel memoirs, comments, vacation galleries.
  • To personalise the feed, remember your language, and surface destinations you love.
  • To detect spam, protect discussions, and keep the community hospitable.
  • To answer messages you send through feedback or the Help Centre.

3. Photographs & memoirs

The vacation photographs and memoirs you publish are yours. NeverBeen holds a limited licence purely to store, resize and display them to the audience you picked (the whole community, or members who follow you). Delete them and the licence ends — backups expire on our rolling retention schedule within 30 days.

We do not run facial recognition, we do not train identification models, and we do not license your images to third parties for advertising.

4. Cookies & tokens

Authentication is encrypted and secure. Sign-in tokens are kept in protected browser storage so you stay logged in, and small preference cookies remember things like your selected language. You can clear them at any time from your browser — you will simply be asked to sign in again.

🍪

Two kinds of cookies, nothing sneaky: essential cookies that make sign-in work, and preference cookies for language and display choices. No advertising trackers, no cross-site profiling.

5. When we share

We share information only in these narrow situations:

  • With your consent — when you explicitly connect or import something.
  • With processors — infrastructure partners bound by contracts that forbid any other use.
  • For legal compliance — when a valid law, court order or rights protection requires it.
  • Never for ads — your personal data is not a product we sell.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, CCPA and similar laws), you can access, correct, export or erase the personal data NeverBeen holds about you, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent never costs you features.

7. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit, access to production systems is limited and logged, and credentials are hashed — NeverBeen staff cannot read your identity-provider password because it never reaches us. If we ever learn of an incident that affects you, we will tell you promptly and clearly.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy, your data, or a privacy request? The fastest path is the Help Centre; for anything account-specific write to us through the feedback form and the studio will respond within a few working days.
